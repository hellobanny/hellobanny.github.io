Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to Oddkit (偶用) for iOS. The app is developed and maintained by an individual developer. We keep your data on your device wherever possible: the app has no account system, no servers operated by us, and your personal information is not uploaded to us.

1. Information the App Processes

The app processes the following information on your device only after you use a feature and agree to the system permission prompt:

  • Camera: used for AR measurement, the live compass, the camera level, the mirror in the dark, and party draw photos. Frames stay on your device and are not uploaded. The only photo saved is a site photo you choose to keep in a report.
  • Location: used for solar calculations, compass direction and nearby places. The app requests While Using access only and does not track you in the background. You can also enter coordinates manually.
  • Contacts: used to calculate pinyin initials from Chinese names and write them into the name field you choose. Original values are saved so you can restore them in one tap. Contacts stay on your device and are not uploaded.
  • Photos: the app reads only the single image you choose through the system photo picker. It does not browse your library.
  • Motion and orientation: used to detect device posture for level guides and direction.
  • Purchase status: the app reads only the entitlement status provided by Apple to determine whether features are unlocked. It never sees your payment card or billing details.

This information is used only to provide the relevant features. It is not used for advertising, profiling or model training.

2. Use and Retention

Feature data is stored in the app's local data directory until you delete it or uninstall the app. Saved contact originals are kept until you restore them or uninstall the app. We do not sell, rent or disclose your personal information to unrelated third parties.

3. Permissions

The app requests permissions only when you use a feature that needs them. You can turn off Camera, Location, Contacts and similar permissions at any time in iOS Settings → Oddkit. Denying a permission affects only that feature and does not block the other tools.

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses Apple system services for some features:

  • When you search for nearby places or a place name, your current location may be sent to Apple Maps and handled under Apple's privacy policy.
  • Purchases and restore purchases are handled by Apple.
  • If you enable iCloud Contacts sync or device backup, the relevant data is handled by Apple.

Other than these cases and where required by law, we do not disclose your personal information to third parties. The app contains no advertising, third-party analytics or data-mining SDK.

5. Data Security

Feature data is stored in the iOS app sandbox and protected by system data protection. We operate no servers and have no back office that can view your feature data.

6. Your Rights

You can view and edit contact fields, delete reports inside the feature, or turn permissions off in system settings. Uninstalling the app clears the feature data stored on your device. If you have questions about this policy, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

Oddkit is a general-purpose utility. It is not specifically designed for children and does not collect children's personal information. If a guardian finds that a child has used a permission-protected feature without consent, they can turn the permission off in system settings or uninstall the app.

8. Changes to This Policy

If features or data practices change, we will update this policy and release it with an app version. For material changes, we will notify you in the app or in the release notes.
