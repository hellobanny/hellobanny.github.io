Privacy Policy

Calc24 - Swipe to Solve 24 Math Puzzles
Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to Calc24 ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience while using our App. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

We want to be transparent about the information we collect. The App is designed to work primarily offline, and we minimize data collection to only what is necessary to provide and improve our services.

How We Use Your Information

  • Core Functionality: To provide and maintain the App's core functionality, including game progress, level completion, and high scores stored locally on your device.
  • Improvement: To improve the App's performance, user experience, and develop new features based on aggregated, anonymized usage patterns.
  • Support: To provide customer support and respond to your inquiries, feedback, and requests.
  • Compatibility: To ensure the App functions correctly across different devices and iOS versions.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share anonymized, aggregated data with analytics partners to help us understand how the App is used and improve our services. This data cannot be used to identify you personally.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Your game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage mechanisms. We recommend keeping your device secure with a passcode or biometric authentication.

Your Rights

  • Access: You can view all your stored data within the App, including game progress and high scores.
  • Deletion: You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the App from your device.
  • Contact: You can contact us at any time with questions or concerns about your privacy.

Children's Privacy

The App is designed for users of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App functions primarily offline and does not require account creation or personal information to use. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child, please contact us and we will promptly delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how we protect your information.

Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at hellobanny@gmail.com. We will respond to your inquiry as soon as possible.
